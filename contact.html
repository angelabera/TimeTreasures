Frequently Asked Questions

Are your artifacts authentic?

All our pieces are carefully crafted replicas using traditional methods and materials. We provide detailed historical documentation with each item, and clearly label all products as high-quality reproductions.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all items. Items must be returned in original condition with all packaging and documentation. Custom orders are non-returnable unless there's a manufacturing defect.

Do you offer certificates of authenticity?

Yes! Every purchase comes with a detailed certificate that includes historical context, craftsmanship details, and materials used. This ensures you understand the provenance and quality of your piece.

Can you create custom pieces?

Absolutely! We work with skilled artisans to create custom historical replicas. Contact us with your specific requirements, and we'll provide a quote and timeline for your unique piece.

How do you ensure historical accuracy?

Our team includes professional historians and archaeologists who research each piece extensively. We use museum-quality references and work with experts in specific historical periods to ensure accuracy.

What are your shipping options?

We offer standard shipping (5-7 business days), expedited shipping (2-3 business days), and overnight delivery. All items are carefully packaged to ensure safe arrival. Free shipping on orders over $200.
